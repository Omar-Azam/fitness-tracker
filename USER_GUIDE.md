## 1. Getting Started

### Creating a New Account
1. Open the application in your web browser.
2. Click **"Get Started"** on the landing page or navigate to **"Register"** in the top navigation bar.
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name** (optional, e.g. *Alex Johnson*)
   - **Username** (required, min 3 characters, e.g. *alexj*)
   - **Email Address** (required, e.g. *alex@example.com*)
   - **Password** (required, min 8 characters)
4. Click **"Register"**. You will automatically be logged in and taken to your personal **Dashboard**.

### Signing In
1. Click **"Sign In"** at the top right of the screen.
2. Enter your **Email or Username** and your **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.

## 2. Using the Dashboard

Once logged in, the **Dashboard** serves as your primary fitness command center:

- **Weekly Stats Overview**:
  - **Workouts This Week**: See how many workout sessions you have completed during the current week.
  - **Nutrition Logged**: See how many days this week you have logged meals.
  - **Logged Logs Total**: Total historical progress entries.
- **Weight Progress Sparkline**: A quick visual snapshot of your recent weight changes over time.
- **Recent Workouts Feed**: Lists your latest logged workout routines with direct links to view all.
- **Recent Meals Feed**: Shows your most recently logged meals and quick access to today's food log.

## 3. Logging & Managing Workouts

Navigate to the **Workouts** tab from the top navigation bar (or the bottom navigation bar on mobile phones).

### Logging a New Workout
1. Click the green **"+ Log Workout"** button.
2. Fill in the workout details:
   - **Workout Name**: e.g., *Chest & Triceps Hypertrophy* or *Morning 5k Run*.
   - **Category**: Select *Strength*, *Cardio*, *Flexibility & Yoga*, or *Other / Mixed*.
   - **Date**: Defaults to today (or pick any past date).
   - **Duration**: Duration of the session in minutes (e.g., *45*).
   - **Tags**: Comma-separated tags to help you organize (e.g., *chest, push, hypertrophy*).
3. **Add Exercises**:
   - Click **"+ Add Exercise"** to add an exercise row.
   - Enter the **Exercise Name** (e.g., *Bench Press*).
   - Set the number of **Sets**, **Reps**, **Weight** (in kg or lbs), and any optional exercise notes.
   - You can add as many exercises as you like.
4. Click **"Save Workout"**. A success notification will appear.

### Filtering & Searching Your Workouts
- **Filter by Category**: Select *Strength*, *Cardio*, etc. from the category dropdown to see only specific workout types.
- **Filter by Tag**: Type in a tag name (e.g., *legs*) to quickly find matching routines.
- **Date Filters**: Filter by a custom date range (*From* and *To* dates).

### Editing or Deleting a Workout
- On any workout card, click the **Pencil icon (Edit)** to modify the workout or exercises.
- Click the **Trash icon (Delete)** to remove a workout. A confirmation prompt will ask you to confirm before deleting to prevent accidental removals.

### Exporting Workouts
- Click the **"Export"** button at the top right of the Workouts page and select either **Export CSV** (for spreadsheets like Excel) or **Export PDF** (for a printable formatted report).

## 4. Tracking Daily Meals & Nutrition

Navigate to the **Nutrition** tab to track your daily food intake and calories.

### Navigating Days
- Use the **Date Selector** at the top of the page:
  - Click the **Left Arrow** or **Right Arrow** to browse past or future days.
  - Click the date picker to jump directly to any specific date.
  - Click the **"Today"** badge to immediately return to today's log.

### Reviewing Daily Totals & Macros
- The banner at the top shows your total daily calorie intake (`kcal`) alongside three macronutrient breakdown gauges:
  - 🔵 **Protein (g)**
  - 🟡 **Carbohydrates (g)**
  - 🔴 **Fat (g)**

### Logging a Meal
1. Under **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner**, or **Snacks**, click the **"+ Add"** button.
2. Enter your food items:
   - **Food Item Name**: e.g., *Oatmeal with Blueberries*.
   - **Quantity & Unit**: e.g., *1 bowl* or *200 grams*.
   - **Calories & Macros**: Enter calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
   - Click **"+ Add Food Item"** to log multiple foods in the same meal (e.g. eggs + toast + coffee).
3. Click **"Save Entry"**. Your daily summary cards will update in real time.

### Quick-Add (Duplicating Meals)
- If you frequently eat the same meals, click the **Copy icon (Duplicate)** on any past meal entry. This pre-fills the form so you can log your favorite meals in a single click!

### Exporting Nutrition Logs
- Click the **"Export"** button at the top right of the Nutrition page to download your meals as a **CSV spreadsheet** or **PDF summary**.

## 5. Monitoring Progress & Trend Charts

Navigate to the **Progress** tab to track body measurements and physical milestones.

### Logging a New Progress Entry
1. Click **"Log New Entry"**.
2. Enter your data:
   - **Date**: Date of measurement.
   - **Body Weight**: Current weight in kg or lbs.
   - **Body Measurements**: Chest, Waist, Hips, Arms, and Thighs (in cm or inches).
   - **Custom Athletic Metrics**: Track custom milestones like *"5k Run Time"* (e.g., *24 mins*), *"Bench Press 1RM"* (e.g., *100 kg*), or *"Body Fat %"*.
3. Click **"Save Log"**.

### Reading Your Progress
- **Body Measurements Grid**: Shows your most recent measurements for each body part alongside the percentage change (+ or -) compared to your previous entry.
- **Weight Progression Chart**: An interactive line chart displaying your weight trend over time.
- **Custom Performance Trends**: Use the metric dropdown to switch between any measurement or custom performance metric to visualize your athletic improvements.

## 6. Global Search & Notifications

### Real-Time Search Bar
- Located in the top header (or via the search icon on mobile).
- Type any keyword (e.g., *squat*, *protein*, *hypertrophy*) to instantly search across all your logged workouts and food entries.
- Click any result in the dropdown to navigate directly to it.

### Milestone Notifications
- When you complete **3 or more workouts in a single week**, the app automatically awards you a **"Goal Achieved"** milestone!
- Click the **Bell Icon** in the top navigation bar to view your notifications.
- Click on any notification to mark it as read.

## 7. Settings & Customization

Navigate to the **Settings** page (gear icon) to configure personal preferences:

- **Profile Details**: Update your full name or add a profile picture URL.
- **Units of Measurement**:
  - **Metric System**: Kilograms (kg), Centimeters (cm), Kilometers (km).
  - **Imperial System**: Pounds (lbs), Inches (in), Miles (mi).
  - Changing this automatically updates units across your Progress charts and logs.
- **Interface Theme**:
  - **Dark Mode**: High-contrast slate theme with glowing accents.
  - **Light Mode**: Crisp, bright aesthetic.
- **Notifications Toggle**: Enable or disable milestone notifications.
- Click **"Save All Settings"** to persist your preferences.
